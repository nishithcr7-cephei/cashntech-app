# Dealer Finance Program - Pharma Supply Chain Finance

## 🏭 PHARMA SUPPLY CHAIN - THE PLAYERS

```
Raw Material Suppliers (Vendors)
          ↓
    Manufacturers (Anchors)
    (Sun Pharma, Cipla, etc.)
          ↓
    Distributors (Dealers)
    (Wholesale medicine stockists)
          ↓
    Retailers (Chemists/Pharmacies)
          ↓
    End Customers (Patients)
```

---

## 💊 DEALER FINANCE PROGRAM KYA HAI?

### Simple Terms Mein

Jab ek **Dealer** (distributor) manufacturer se medicines purchase karta hai, toh use immediate payment karna padta hai, lekin wo medicines 30-90 days baad retailers ko bechta hai aur payment milta hai.

**Problem:**
- Dealer ke paas **cash flow gap** hota hai
- Medicines ka stock purchase karne ke liye **working capital** chahiye

**Solution: Dealer Finance Program**
- **Anchor** (Manufacturer) apne trusted dealers ko financing facilitate karta hai
- **Financing Company** (CashnTech) dealer ko instant payment deta hai
- Dealer installments mein repay karta hai

## 📋 KEY TERMS (PHARMA SUPPLY CHAIN FINANCE)

### 1. Anchor (Primary Party)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kaun** | Pharma manufacturer (e.g., Sun Pharma, Cipla, Dr. Reddy's) |
| **Role** | Central financing hub jo apne dealers ko support karta hai |
| **Benefit** | Sales increase (dealers zyada stock le sakte hain) |

### 2. Dealer/Distributor (Counter Party)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kaun** | Wholesale medicine stockist |
| **Role** | Manufacturer se bulk mein medicines kharidta hai |
| **Benefit** | Instant cash milta hai bina bank loan ke |

### 3. Invoice Discounting

**Kya hai:** Dealer apna unpaid invoice financing company ko bechta hai

**Process:**
- Dealer ne ₹10 lakh ki medicines becheen retailer ko
- Payment 60 days baad milega
- Financing company abhi ₹9.5 lakh deta hai (discount ke saath)
- 60 days baad retailer payment karta hai directly financing company ko

### 4. PID (Purchase Invoice Discounting)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Full Form** | Purchase Invoice Discounting |
| **Kya hai** | Dealer manufacturer se purchase karte waqt financing leta hai |

**Example:**
- Dealer ko ₹50 lakh ki medicines chahiye Sun Pharma se
- Dealer ke paas sirf ₹10 lakh hai
- CashnTech ₹40 lakh finance karta hai
- Dealer medicines le leta hai aur monthly installments mein repay karta hai

### 5. SID (Sales Invoice Discounting)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Full Form** | Sales Invoice Discounting |
| **Kya hai** | Dealer retailers ko bechne ke baad financing leta hai |

**Example:**
- Dealer ne ₹30 lakh ki medicines retailers ko becheen
- Payment 90 days baad milega
- CashnTech abhi ₹28 lakh deta hai
- 90 days baad retailers payment karte hain

### 6. OD (Overdraft)

**Kya hai:** Pre-approved credit limit jo dealer use kar sakta hai

**Example:**
- Dealer ko ₹1 crore ka OD limit mila
- Zaroorat padne par wo limit se paise withdraw kar sakta hai
- Interest sirf use kiye gaye amount pe lagta hai

### 7. Credit Account

**Kya hai:** Dealer ka financing account

**Contains:**
- Available credit limit
- Used amount
- Outstanding balance
- Repayment schedule

### 8. DPD (Days Past Due)

**Kya hai:** Overdue payment tracking

**Example:**
- Payment due date: January 15
- Aaj ki date: February 15
- **DPD = 31 days**

**Categories:**
- **0-30 DPD:** Minor delay
- **31-60 DPD:** Moderate risk
- **60+ DPD:** High risk

### 9. Anchor Credit

**Kya hai:** Manufacturer ki guarantee pe financing

**How:** Sun Pharma guarantee deta hai ke "ye dealer trusted hai"

**Benefit:** Dealer ko better interest rates milte hain

### 10. Financing Request

**Kya hai:** Dealer apply karta hai naye finance ke liye

**Process:**
1. Dealer: "Mujhe ₹20 lakh chahiye Cipla se medicines kharidne ke liye"
2. Checker: Verifies request
3. Anchor: Approves
4. CashnTech: Disburses amount

## 🔄 REAL-WORLD PHARMA EXAMPLE

**Scenario:** Shree Radhe Medicines (Dealer)

### Step 1: Purchase (PID)
- Sun Pharma se ₹1 crore ki medicines order ki
- **Dealer Maker:** Invoice create kiya
- **Dealer Checker:** Approve kiya
- **CashnTech:** Documents verify kiye
- **Anchor (Sun Pharma):** Final approval
- **Result:** ₹1 crore disbursed to dealer

### Step 2: Stock Management
- Dealer ne medicines apne warehouse mein stock kiye
- Retailers ko supply shuru ki

### Step 3: Sales (SID)
- 50 retailers ko ₹80 lakh ki medicines becheen
- Payment terms: 60 days
- Dealer immediately ₹75 lakh discount karwa ke le sakta hai

### Step 4: Repayment
- Monthly installments mein original loan (₹1 crore) repay karna hai
- Interest + principal combined EMI

### Step 5: Overdraft Usage
- Emergency mein agar ₹10 lakh chahiye urgent purchase ke liye
- OD limit se withdraw kar sakta hai
- Next month repay kar sakta hai

## 📊 DEALER CHECKER KA ROLE (Pharma Context)

### Daily Tasks
- **Invoice Review:** Dealer Maker ne jo invoices create kiye, unhe verify karna
- **Credit Monitoring:** Available limit check karna
- **Payment Tracking:** Pending payments ka status dekhna
- **DPD Management:** Overdue invoices ko track karna
- **Anchor Communication:** Sun Pharma/Cipla se coordination

### Approval Decisions
- ✅ Invoice amount sahi hai?
- ✅ Medicine batch numbers correct hain?
- ✅ Credit limit available hai?
- ✅ Documents complete hain (GST invoice, delivery challan)?
